I received the **2023 Qualcomm Innovation Fellowship - North America**.

The Qualcomm Innovation Fellowship (QIF) supports PhD students pursuing research in mobile and related technologies. Fellowship recipients receive research support and mentorship from Qualcomm engineers.

My fellowship proposal focused on research in unary computing for energy-efficient AI accelerators.
